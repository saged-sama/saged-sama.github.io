<p>English360 is an English learning platform founded by my favorite educator, <strong>Mynul Islam Rudra Bhai</strong>, whose work has helped build one of the fastest-growing academic tutoring platforms.</p><p>I first got to know him through this project, where I contributed to establishing and developing English360's online presence through its website.</p><p>One of my major contributions was building what I consider one of the most sophisticated and cheapest online exercise arenas for students available in Bangladesh. It allows learners to practice freely using questions from actual public examination papers, submit their answers, and receive automated evaluations and insights powered by an <strong>AI Agent</strong>.</p><p>The goal was to go beyond a conventional educational website and create an interactive learning environment where students can practice, receive feedback, and improve through AI-assisted assessment.</p>
